The Story 展廳故事

Just a few minutes from our classrooms stands one of the most important museums in Asia: the Southern Branch of the National Palace Museum, in Taibao City. While the original museum in Taipei is famous for Chinese treasures, the Southern Branch has a wider mission. It is an Asian Art and Culture Museum — a place that tells the story of the whole continent.

離我們教室只要幾分鐘，就矗立著亞洲最重要的博物館之一：位於太保市的「國立故宮博物院南部院區」。臺北的故宮以中國文物聞名，南院的使命卻更寬廣——它是一座「亞洲藝術文化博物館」，述說整個亞洲大陸的故事。

The museum opened in 2015. Its address — 888 Gugong Boulevard — sits beside the high-speed rail station and the seat of Chiayi County. For an international traveller stepping off the train, the Southern Branch is often their first impression of southern Taiwan. That is exactly why a docent — a trained guide who explains the artworks — matters so much.

南院於 2015 年開幕，地址「故宮大道 888 號」就在高鐵站與嘉義縣治旁邊。對一位剛下車的國際旅客來說，南院往往是他們對台灣南部的第一印象——這正是為什麼一位受過訓練、能解說作品的「導覽員（docent）」如此重要。

In this course you will become a young docent. You will not memorise everything; instead you will learn how to introduce one gallery, describe one object, and answer a visitor’s question — all in clear, friendly English. By the final lesson you will give a short tour of your own.

在這門課裡，你會成為一位小小導覽員。你不必背下所有東西，而是學會如何用清楚、友善的英文「介紹」一個展廳、描述一件文物、回答訪客的提問。到最後一課，你將親自帶一段簡短的導覽。

“A docent is not someone who knows everything. A docent is someone who helps you see.”導覽員不是無所不知的人，而是幫助你「看見」的人。

branch (n.)

a person who guides visitors and explains the artworks in a museum
（博物館）導覽員
“Our docent explained the painting in simple English.”
exhibit (n.)
an object or collection shown to the public in a museum

gallery (n.)
a room or hall where artworks are displayed
展廳；藝廊

collection (n.)

Check

Quick Check 小測驗

Choose one answer for each question. 每題選一個答案，作答後會看到解析。

Q1.What kind of museum is the Southern Branch?
南院是哪一種博物館？
The Southern Branch is positioned as an Asian Art and Culture Museum — broader than the Taipei museum's focus on Chinese treasures.南院定位為「亞洲藝術文化博物館」，視野比臺北故宮的中國文物更寬廣。
Q2.A docent is best described as someone who ___.
「docent」最適合的描述是？
A docent is a trained guide who helps visitors understand what they are seeing.docent 是受過訓練的導覽員，幫助訪客理解眼前的作品。
Q3.Which sentence would you say to begin a tour?
開始導覽時你會說哪一句？
You open a tour with a welcome and by introducing yourself as the guide.導覽的開場是先歡迎訪客，並介紹自己是今天的導覽員。
Q4.In which year did the Southern Branch open?
南院哪一年開幕？
The Southern Branch opened in 2015 in Taibao City, beside the high-speed rail.南院於 2015 年在太保市、高鐵站旁開幕。
